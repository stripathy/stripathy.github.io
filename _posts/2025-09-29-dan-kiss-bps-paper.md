Congrats to Dan Kiss, Xiaolin Zhou, and Nicole Endresz on the publication of their paper "Cortical GABAergic Neuron Dysregulation in Schizophrenia Is Age Dependent" in Biological Psychiatry: Global Open Science! The study analyzed transcriptomic data from over 1,400 individuals and found that reductions in key inhibitory neuron types in schizophrenia — particularly parvalbumin and somatostatin cells — are most pronounced in younger individuals, highlighting the importance of considering age in studies of this disorder.

We went out to celebrate with Dan at Prenup Pub near UofT campus.
