Huge congrats to Nuo Xu and Katherine Rynard on their co-first author paper, [Long-read proteogenomic atlas of human neuronal differentiation reveals isoform diversity informing neurodevelopmental risk mechanisms](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-76675-1), now out in Nature Communications! I'm personally really proud of this one, as it was our lab's first foray into long-read RNA-seq and proteomics. Nuo and Katherine led this really exciting project from start to finish.

To celebrate the paper's acceptance, Nuo and Katherine popped the champagne, and Howard Lipshitz gave a speech about the trajectory of the paper and the overall effort behind it, which was originally funded by the SFARI foundation.
