We recently celebrated Thanksgiving at lab manager Ellie's family farm! We had a really nice time getting to see the farm's baby ducks, goats, and cows, followed by a really nice Thanksgiving lunch.

Huge thanks to Ellie's family for hosting the lab and our families, and to all of the lab members for preparing the food — especially Thomas, Nicole, and Ellie's family for preparing the turkey and fixings!
